## Cadence and follow-up

- **Send one pitch per day** starting the morning after launch. HN / Reddit / LinkedIn / X go up first; newsletters come the next day when they can point to real traction.
- **Order:** Simon Willison (fast operator, sets pattern), Nathan Lambert (fast, K3 angle), Latent Space (thoughtful), Rundown / Ben's Bites / TLDR (mass-market AI), then the two film outlets last (IndieWire, No Film School — they're the ones most likely to pick up on cadence).
- **Follow-up:** one polite ping after 10 business days if no reply. One final ping 3 weeks later ONLY if the project has new news.
- **Track outcomes:** simple spreadsheet — outlet, date sent, date replied, outcome.

## The differentiator to preserve across all 8 pitches

Every pitch leads with the specific angle that outlet's audience will find newsworthy:

- Simon Willison → the anti-AI-slop framing
- Nathan Lambert → the K3 refuses-to-recommend-when-worse angle
- Latent Space → skills as packaging format for procedure
- Rundown / Ben's Bites / TLDR → 49-skill breadth + Film Pack novelty
- IndieWire Craft → console-based indie stack
- No Film School → Movie Magic alternative

Don't lead with the workhorse metaphor. It reads inside the brand but doesn't land cold. Save the metaphor for the body of the launch post, not the cold pitch.
