# Example — Secondary purchase with mid-process repricing

**Situation.** A family office (via an SPV) is buying 40,000 common shares of a late-stage private company from an early employee (via a broker-dealer). Between the signed term sheet and the actual closing, the issuer completes a new priced round at a lower valuation. The broker attempts to hold the original PPS; the buyer refuses and reprices the second tranche. First tranche funds; second tranche is delayed, then closes at a lower price. A post-closing fee dispute between the broker and a co-introducer follows.

**Deliverable target.** Two-page executive memo per the standard structure.

---

## Title
Acme Robotics Secondary — Transaction Summary

## Executive Summary
Family Office A, acting through its wholly-owned Delaware LLC "SPV-A," agreed to purchase 40,000 shares of Acme Robotics common stock from Seller S, a founding engineer, in a two-tranche private secondary intermediated by Broker B (a registered broker-dealer) with introduction by Introducer I. The original term sheet set price at $80.00 per share for a $3,200,000 gross purchase. Between term-sheet execution and second-tranche funding, Acme completed a Series F priced round at $64.00 per share. Buyer refused to close the second tranche at the original PPS. After 46 days of negotiation, the second tranche closed at $68.00 per share. Both tranches settled and shares transferred. A post-closing dispute between Broker B and Introducer I over fee-split allocation remains unresolved as of the record's last date.

## How the Transaction Developed

**Original transaction (2026-04-10 to 2026-05-01).** Introducer I connected Family Office A and Seller S. Broker B was engaged to intermediate. Term sheet executed 2026-05-01 at $80.00 PPS, 40,000 shares, two equal tranches, 3% broker fee borne by Seller.

**First tranche and diligence (2026-05-01 to 2026-05-14).** Diligence completed; issuer ROFR waived; transfer form countersigned; first tranche wire funded 2026-05-14; shares transferred on-record 2026-05-19.

**Issuer repricing event (2026-05-30).** Acme announced closing of Series F at $64.00 PPS. Buyer's counsel emailed Broker B same day proposing to reprice second tranche.

**Repricing negotiation (2026-05-30 to 2026-06-25).** Broker B initially resisted, citing signed term sheet. Buyer's position: material adverse change to secondary market pricing. Compromise reached at $68.00 PPS on 2026-06-25 (halfway between original $80 and Series F reference of $64, minus a 6.25% liquidity premium for the secondary).

**Second tranche and closing (2026-06-25 to 2026-06-29).** Amended term sheet executed 2026-06-26. Second tranche funded 2026-06-29 at $68.00 PPS. Shares transferred on-record 2026-07-02.

## Why the Transaction Became Difficult

1. **Issuer priced round mid-process.** A $64 Series F between tranches invalidated the pricing thesis of the original term sheet.
2. **No repricing mechanism in the term sheet.** The original term sheet lacked an MFN or repricing clause tied to primary issuer events. Both sides negotiated from a blank.
3. **Broker fee tied to gross.** Broker B's 3% was calculated on gross purchase, giving the broker a direct economic interest in preserving the $80 PPS. This colored the negotiation.
4. **Introducer role ambiguous.** Introducer I claimed a fee-split arrangement with Broker B under a prior verbal understanding. Broker B disputed. See separate section.

## Fee or Commercial Dispute

**Introducer I's position (per email 2026-07-14):** Introducer I asserts that in a phone call on 2026-04-08, Broker B agreed to a 40% split of the 3% broker fee ($35,532 of $88,800 total on final blended economics) in exchange for the introduction. Introducer I has no written record of this agreement.

**Broker B's position (per email 2026-07-16):** Broker B acknowledges an "introduction fee discussion" but asserts no specific split was agreed and that "customary practice" for a one-touch introduction is a fixed $10,000 finder's fee, which Broker B has offered to pay.

**What would resolve.** A contemporaneous email, text message, or written engagement letter from between 2026-04-01 and 2026-04-15 confirming or refuting a 40% split. Introducer I has been asked to search for such a record; no such record has been produced as of the last entry in the record (2026-07-22).

## Bottom Line

The Acme Robotics secondary closed. Buyer received 40,000 shares of Acme common on-record; Seller received $2,871,200 net of broker fees. Blended PPS was $74.00, materially below the original term sheet's $80.00 due to a mid-process issuer repricing event. The remaining unresolved item is the fee-split dispute between Broker B and Introducer I; resolution would require either a contemporaneous written record of the alleged 40% split or a settlement between the two intermediaries. The securities transaction itself is complete and does not appear to be at further risk.
