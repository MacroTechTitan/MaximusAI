# Example: $1M indie feature top-sheet

## Situation

- 14-day shoot, 96-page screenplay
- SAG Modified Low, non-union crew
- Regional shoot (single primary location, 3 satellite locations)
- Director-producer team, one hired department heads

## Notes on this budget

- **Cast fringes:** SAG Modified Low carries ~15.5% pension & health plus ~7.5% payroll tax. Actual ~23-25%, blended with lower non-union crew fringes to reach the 32% weighted figure.
- **No completion bond:** at $1M, most producers self-insure the completion risk rather than pay a bond. Line item is shown for financiers who require it; strike from top sheet if not applicable.
- **Contingency:** 10% is the norm. Some financiers accept 7%, some require 12-15%.
- **Zero-value lines** (Second Unit, Aerial & Underwater) stay in the top sheet as `$0` rather than being dropped. Financiers reading the top sheet want to see the producer considered every category.

## What we did not do

- Did not set any of these numbers. Every dollar figure came from the line producer's bids and rate cards.
- Did not commit the producer to any specific SAG contract tier — that's a producer decision with the film's SAG representative.
- Did not include any tax-incentive rebate math. That's a separate document (state film office application + tax counsel opinion).
- Did not book any crew, negotiate any deals, or contact any vendors.

## Handoff note

**"This top-sheet is a v1.0 draft based on the line producer's initial bids. Verify against final vendor quotes, SAG contract selection, and insurance carrier package before presenting to financiers. Not final until line producer and UPM sign off."**
